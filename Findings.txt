Flagging products with nutriscore unknown or not applicable
Sugar and nutriscore are strongly correlated in beverages — as sugar goes up, grade gets worse. This validates the dataset quality and also shows that the unknown products with 32g sugar are likely data quality issues worth flagging — we can use AI to identify and highlight incomplete or suspicious entries.

Key findings from product data

Solution:
